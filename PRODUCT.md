# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A developer on macOS opens the dashboard from the menu bar to triage routine findings and manage scheduled checks.

## Product Purpose

Show what needs the user's attention, whose turn it is, and how recently routines checked their sources.

## Brand Personality

Native, quiet, practical. Preserve the direction in design/DIRECTION.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, card grids, colored stripes, and badges on everything.

## Design Principles

- Answer the user's next question at a glance.
- Group findings by who acts next.
- Keep routine state and evidence visible.
- Preserve compact lists and keyboard navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, light and dark appearance, reduced motion, and text labels alongside status colors.
